Privacy Policy

How Retro日和 handles information received through orders, inquiries and wholesale requests.

Information we may collect

Name, email address, telephone number, shipping address, company or store name, order details, inquiry details and other information necessary to provide the service.

Purposes of use

Stock confirmation, order handling, payment instructions, shipping, customer support, wholesale proposals, transaction-related communication and service improvement.

Form submission

Order and inquiry forms use an external form-delivery service to forward information to our receiving mailbox. Information entered in the form passes through that service when submitted.

Order basket

The basket uses browser local storage to remember selected works. Adding a work to the basket alone does not send your name, address or other personal information to us. Information is transmitted when you submit the order form.

Third-party disclosure

We do not disclose personal information for unrelated purposes except where required by law, necessary for shipping or transaction processing, or with the individual’s consent.

Security and handling

Information is handled only to the extent needed for orders and inquiries and is not unnecessarily made public.
