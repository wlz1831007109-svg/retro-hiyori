WORK TYPE

Hand-painted and craft-produced.

HAND MADE

Hand-painted / hand-written

Works made by hand using brush, ink, pigments or related materials. Such works are explicitly labeled on the product page.

CRAFT PRODUCTION

Craft-produced work

Works completed through production methods other than hand painting or hand writing. An artist name does not mean the named artist personally painted the item.

PAYMENT & DELIVERY

FAQ
If an artist’s name appears, is it an authentic work by that artist?

Not necessarily. Names may reflect inscriptions, designs or attributions. Unless a specific guarantee is stated, Retro日和 does not treat an unauthenticated work as a certified original.

Can I search only for hand-painted works?

Yes. As the collection grows, production category can be used to distinguish hand-painted works from craft-produced works. Check the current works page for availability.

Should I transfer payment immediately after ordering?

No. Please wait until we have confirmed stock and emailed you the bank-transfer details.

Can I source a large quantity?

Yes. Use the wholesale page and tell us quantity, budget, use case, desired format and preferred production category.
