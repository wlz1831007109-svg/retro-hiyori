PERIOD GUIDE

Northern Song

960–1127

The imperial academy and scholar-official culture developed in parallel. Monumental landscape, detailed bird-and-flower painting and literati ideas made the Northern Song one of the great peaks of Chinese painting.

Monumental landscapeImperial academyScholar-official culture
HOW TO LOOK

Three points to notice.

01

Context

Ask who commissioned, collected or viewed paintings in this period, and how court, religious, scholarly or commercial culture shaped the work.

02

Brush & composition

Look at line, texture, ink density, color, empty space and the construction of depth before focusing only on subject matter.

03

Attribution

Historical Chinese painting survives through originals, copies, later versions and traditional attributions. Treat names and dates as evidence to be evaluated, not automatic certainty.
