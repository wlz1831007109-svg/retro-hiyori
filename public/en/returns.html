RETURNS / CANCELLATION

Returns & Cancellations

Chinese paintings, calligraphy and mounted works may show individual condition differences and age-related changes. Please review photographs, dimensions and production category before ordering.

Before payment

After an order request is submitted, we confirm stock before sending payment instructions. If you have concerns about the confirmed item, shipping cost or delivery conditions, contact us before paying.

Customer-initiated cancellation or return

As a general rule, we do not accept cancellation or return after payment due to change of mind, insufficient size checking or ordering error. Please carefully review the photographs, dimensions and description.

Wrong item or major discrepancy

If a different item arrives, or if there is a serious discrepancy from the published description, contact us as soon as reasonably possible after delivery and include photographs. We will review the situation individually.

Damage in transit

If the parcel or work appears damaged during shipping, keep the packaging and take photographs of the outer box, packing condition and damaged area before contacting us.

Condition of works

Used artworks and mounted works may show stains, folds, dirt, age-related changes or mounting wear. Condition differences that are visible in the photographs or described on the product page may not qualify as a return reason.

Wholesale and business orders

Because quantity and specifications are agreed individually, the quotation and conditions accepted at the time of order take priority.
