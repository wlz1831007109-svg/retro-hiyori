TRANSPARENCY

We do not present uncertainty as certainty.

Artist names are treated as attributions.

When a name comes from an inscription, design source or traditional attribution, it is not presented as proof that the named artist personally painted the work.

Production category is displayed.

Hand-painted / hand-written works are labeled accordingly; other production methods are categorized as craft-produced works.

Unauthenticated works are not guaranteed.

When no expert authentication has been conducted, we do not guarantee a work as an authentic original by a named artist.
