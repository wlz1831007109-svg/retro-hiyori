HISTORY

See Chinese painting through time.

From Six Dynasties figure painting to Song landscape, Yuan literati art, Ming–Qing schools and modern reform, historical context changes the way a work can be read.

Six Dynasties3rd–6th centuries

Line, figure painting and early painting theory.

 Tang618–907

Court figures, Buddhist art, horses and emerging landscape.

 Northern Song960–1127

Monumental landscape, academy painting and literati culture.

 Yuan1271–1368

Calligraphic brushwork and the consolidation of literati painting.

 Ming1368–1644

Wu School, Zhe School, court painters and individual voices.

 Qing1644–1912

Orthodox lineages, individualists, regional schools and court art.

FORMATS

Different forms, different ways of looking.

Chinese painting and calligraphy are not limited to hanging scrolls. Handscrolls, album leaves, screen sets, couplets and unmounted sheets create different viewing experiences.

Hanging scroll

Viewed vertically on a wall, together with mounting and surrounding space.

Handscroll

Opened gradually by hand, turning viewing into a journey through time and space.

Album leaves

Small, intimate pages that reward close attention to brushwork, inscriptions and seals.
